The Baobab Scaling Framework™

Growth Without Losing Soul

A baobab grows from the inside out. Deep roots first, strong trunk second, branches last. Sustainable organisational growth follows the same logic: strengthen the conditions that allow growth before asking the organisation to carry more.

The Growth Gap

A business can have a strong market opportunity and a clear growth strategy while the organisation behind it is not yet ready to carry that growth.
